## customer-360-marketing-attribution
Customer 360 and Marketing Attribution Platform using BigQuery, Tableau, SQL, and synthetic demo data.
Customer 360 & Marketing Attribution Platform
## Project Overview
An end-to-end marketing analytics demonstration that combines customer profiles, order data, advertising spend, and marketing touchpoints to explore customer behavior and campaign performance.
## Objectives
Build a unified Customer 360 view.
Analyze customer recency, frequency, and monetary value.
Compare marketing channel revenue and advertising spend.
Calculate campaign ROI, ROAS, cost per acquisition, and conversions.
Compare first-touch, last-touch, and linear attribution approaches.
